Homebrew showcase

New games for old consoles, made by today's developers. Free to play here.

    Publish your homebrew game here
    1. Put the game in a new folder: homebrew/my-game/ with the ROM (or a zip).
    2. Add a game.json with at least title, author, system, rom and license; add a description, controls, screenshots and links to make the page complete. Or run node tools/new-homebrew.mjs path/to/game.nes --title "My Game" --author "Me" --license MIT, which does both.
    3. Push to GitHub. The workflow rebuilds homebrew/index.json, and the game gets its own page and a Play button.

    Every field is described in homebrew/README.md. Only publish games you made or have permission to share. New releases also appear in the feed (for feed readers).
