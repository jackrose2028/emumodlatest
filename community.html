    Community spaces

    A game corner for a school, library, shelter, hospital ward or waiting room, on an old or donated laptop, Chromebook or tablet. Visitors only see the games you choose, during the hours you set, at a volume you limit, and each visitor starts with fresh saves. Set it up once; then it runs without anything to configure.

    Offline

    This device: "Prepare this device" stores the chosen games and their emulator cores in the browser, so it keeps working with no internet; on a Chromebook or tablet, also install the site (browser menu → Install, or Add to Home Screen). Another device: "Download offline package" makes one zip with the site, the chosen games, their cores and these settings. Unzip it on a laptop and double-click START-COMMUNITY.bat (Windows) or run start-community.sh (Mac, Linux); it needs Python 3, no internet.
