Preservation archive

Catalogue records for public-domain and rights-cleared software: who made it, where the copy came from, the licence that allows sharing it, and checksums your browser checks against the file before you play it.

About this archive and how to add a record

Only software that may be shared is catalogued: public domain, free licences, or written permission from the rights holder (recorded in the record). Each record keeps its provenance: when the copy was made or received, by whom, and how.

Checksums are the record's fingerprint of the file. CRC32, MD5 and SHA-1 are the three that preservation catalogues publish. Your browser computes them from the file it downloads and compares them with the record, so you can see that the copy you play is the copy that was catalogued.

To add a record: make a folder archive/my-record/ with a record.json (fields in archive/README.md), run node tools/generate-archive-index.mjs --write once to record the checksums, and push. The workflow rechecks every file on each push.
