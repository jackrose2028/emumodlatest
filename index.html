Retro games in the browser, running from this site's own files. What would you like to do?

Featured

    ▶ BasicThe full player as it has been: open a ROM or zip, the game library folders, recent games, both engines, 2.5D and all options. Ready

    Use cases

    1. 1 My CollectionYour own game backups in one place on every device you use: add files or whole folders, covers, favourites, play time, and one file to carry the collection and its saves to another device. Ready
    2. 2 Homebrew showcaseA page per new homebrew game, with credits, controls and a one-click play button, for developers to share their work. Ready
    3. 3 Museum and exhibit kioskFull-screen attract mode, an approved game list, time limits and auto-reset for public screens and touch kiosks. Ready
    4. 4 Classroom: how retro games workLessons beside a running game: palettes, sprites, tiles and frame timing made visible, with worksheets. Ready
    5. 5 Web development case studyA guided tour of how this site works (service worker, WebGL, IndexedDB, threads), linked to the reference docs. Ready
    6. 6 Preservation archiveCatalogue records with metadata, sources and checksums, playable in place, for public-domain and cleared software. Ready
    7. 7 Accessible and comfort playOne place for slow motion, rewind, save anywhere, large touch controls, remapping and one-switch play. Ready
    8. 8 Community spacesA locked-down, low-power setup for schools, shelters, hospitals and waiting rooms on old or donated devices. Ready
    9. 9 Events and speedrun practiceShareable game links, practice save points, timers and splits for community nights and practice. Ready
    10. 10 Build your own arcadeA starter kit to brand the site for a creator, a game jam or an educational project. Ready
