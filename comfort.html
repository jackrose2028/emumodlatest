Accessible and comfort play

Make a game easier to see, hear, hold and play. Pick a preset, adjust anything, and the settings are remembered for each game. Everything here works with a keyboard alone, with touch alone, or with a single switch.

Choose a game and press Play.

In the game: Backspace (hold) rewinds, Ctrl+M moves to these settings, - and = change the speed.
