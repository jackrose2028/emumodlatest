## What it is

The Community spaces page: a simple, locked-down game launcher for libraries, schools, shelters and waiting rooms.

## How it works

- Markup for the visitor screens (welcome, game list, turn, closed) and the staff settings behind a PIN.
- `assets/community.js` keeps the hours, quiet hours, turn length and volume limit, and clears saves between visitors.
- It can write an offline package (the site, the games, their cores and the settings in one zip).

## Why it exists

Shared, often old devices need a launcher that runs itself and leaves nothing behind for the next person.
