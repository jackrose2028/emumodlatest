## What it is

The module behind `collection.html`.

## How it works

- Adds files or a whole folder (with a space check), recognises showcase and archive games by SHA-1 and warns about duplicates.
- Shelves, status and notes per game, the player's own covers, Continue playing and Surprise me.
- Select mode with bulk changes and undo; keyboard and controller navigation; export in parts and multi-file import.

## Why it exists

A player's own library, organised their way, on every device.
