## What it is

The module behind `homebrew.html`.

## How it works

- The list: search, system, sort, genre and tag filters, New and Updated badges, author pages and a random game.
- A game page: play in place or full screen, controls for keyboard, controller and touch, a picture viewer and trailer, credits, release notes, the file checked against its checksums, Add to My Collection, Download, Share (the share page) and Embed.
- Reads `homebrew/index.json`.

## Why it exists

Each homebrew game gets a complete, shareable page from one folder.
